# Pre-registration note: Brihat Samhita, all chapters (English, and Sanskrit where available)
Written 21 October 2026. HONEST ORDER OF EVENTS: the search of the whole English text and the reading of the Sanskrit (printed pp. 393-683 only) came first; the Chapter 17 Mercury-Jupiter test was RUN BEFORE this note was written, so it is exploratory, not pre-registered. Treat its results accordingly.

## Search
Every earthquake mention in the English translation (all 106 chapters; keywords earthquake, earth-quake, tremor, quake, trembling/shaking of the earth, subterranean noise) was located: Ch.1 (contents), 3.10, 4 (crescent), 5.63, 5.92, 17.16, 24.25, 32 (all), 33.12, 46.75, 46.84-88. No other chapter mentions earthquakes.
## Rules and status
* 3.10 sunspots -> earthquake: not tested (spots are almost always present; no daily record used).
* 4 crescent 'yoke' -> earthquake within the month: tested earlier (Rule-First section 7).
* 5.63 earthquake during an eclipse -> eclipse recurs in 6-36 months: not an earthquake predictor; not tested.
* 5.92 earthquake within 7 days after an eclipse ends -> rulers die: tested earlier (eclipse within 7 days before the earthquake; null).
* 17.16 Mercury defeated in conjunction with Jupiter -> earthquakes (and suffering of named peoples): NEW, tests C1-C4 below.
* 24.25 earthquake on a Rohini-yoga day -> no rain: outcome is rain, not earthquake; not tested.
* 32 (35 verses): tested earlier (four circles, time of day, felt distance, aftershock lags).
* 33.12 meteors touching Sun or Moon with an earthquake -> foreign rule: no sky rule; not tested.
* 46.75, 83-88 earthquakes as portents; spring, summer, rainy and winter earthquakes called seasonal: no sky rule; Sanskrit pp. 522 and 542 checked.
## New tests (Chapter 17 verse 16; Sanskrit not available in the supplied PDF, English only)
C1 Mercury within 1 deg of Jupiter (planetary war; separation from own ephemeris). C2 same, Mercury the southern planet (verse 17.9 'defeat'). C3 within 5 deg. C4 within 5 deg, Mercury southern. Binomial against the period-matched background (6-hourly, random 0-6 h shift). Bar 0.05/4 = 0.0125. Added afterwards because events cluster in time: an episode-level permutation test (episodes = separate conjunctions; statistic = episodes containing at least one earthquake; 20,000 random shifts).
