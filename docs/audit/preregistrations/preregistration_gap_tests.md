# Pre-registration: gap tests (written 2026-10-20 before running any of them)

Data: 9,368 declustered M5.5+ earthquakes, 1990-2026 (the Experiment 2 Override set). Discovery = before 2008 (4,661 events); holdout = 2008 onward (4,707). Expected frequencies from the 6-hourly background grid, matched to the years of each half. Lahiri sidereal unless stated. Sky positions from the own ephemeris (validated against the astral-ephemeris tool).
A test passes only if holdout p < threshold AND the discovery half shows the same direction (ratio above 1 for excess).

## Primary family (m = 20; threshold 0.05/20 = 0.0025)
G1  Three or more of Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn in one nakshatra (PT ch.15 formation) - sidereal and tropical (2 tests)
G2  Venus's six circles (PT ch.9; Adya 1-4, Rohita 5-8, Daruna 9-12, Virocana 13-16, Urdhvadanda 17-21, Tiksna 22-26, Ashwini = none), chi-square - sidereal and tropical (2 tests)
G3  Mars stationary (daily motion under 0.05 deg/day); Saturn in Krittika with Jupiter in Vishakha (2 tests)
G4  Any two of the five planets within 3 deg of exact opposition (180), square (90), or trine (120): one test each (3 tests)
G5  Rahu's nakshatra (mean node, 27 classes, chi-square); Moon within 12 deg of the Rahu-Ketu axis (2 tests)
G6  Moon in the same nakshatra as any of the five planets; Moon in the same nakshatra as Mars or Saturn (2 tests)
G7  Combustion (Mars 17, Mercury 14, Jupiter 11, Venus 10, Saturn 15 deg from the Sun): each of the five planets combust (5 tests); two or more planets combust (1 test)
G9  Affliction score = number of 9 harmful conditions present (Moon in sharp group; Moon in Rikta tithi; Mars retrograde; Saturn retrograde; Moon within 5 deg of Mars or Saturn; Mars combust; Saturn combust; Moon within 12 deg of the node axis; Moon in the same nakshatra as Mars or Saturn), equal weights. Test: mean score of events vs background, one-sided (1 test)

## Secondary: two-condition screen (m = 91; threshold 0.05/91 = 0.00055)
All pairs (AND) of 14 base conditions: Moon in sharp group; Rikta tithi; within 1 tithi of new/full moon; eclipse within 15 days; Mars, Saturn, Jupiter, Mercury, Venus retrograde; any planet pair within 3 deg; Moon within 5 deg of a planet/Sun; any planet combust; Moon within 12 deg of node axis; Moon in the same nakshatra as Mars or Saturn. Same pass rule.

## Not testable (recorded, not run)
Heliacal rising/setting beyond the combustion orbs; planet next to a named star (yogatara positions not available); omens and effects.
