# Pre-registration (written 2026-10-20 before fetching the new data): follow-up to the bottom-up scan

Lead from the bottom-up scan of 1,270 declustered M6.5+ events, 1990-2026: Sun in Vishakha (sidereal, Lahiri; Sun sidereal longitude 200.00-213.33 deg, about 16-29 November) had 74 events vs 45.0 expected (1.64x), consistent in three eras.

Single confirmatory test, on an INDEPENDENT catalogue: USGS M6.5+ worldwide, 1964-01-01 to 1989-12-31, declustered with the same Gardner-Knopoff rule.
- Statistic: number of main shocks with the Sun in the 200.00-213.33 deg sidereal window (Lahiri ayanamsa = 23.853 + 0.01397*(year-2000) deg).
- Null: share of days of the year in the window (daily grid over 1964-1989).
- One-sided upper-tail binomial, p < 0.05 required, in the direction of excess.
- Secondary (reported, not used for the verdict): the same window in M>=7.0 events only; Sun position computed with a standard low-precision solar formula (error about 0.01 deg).
