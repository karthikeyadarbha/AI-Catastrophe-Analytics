The first work that Stiryadeva wrote was a commentary on the
Mahabhaskartya-vyakhya of Govinda-svami.| He mentions this fact,
as indicated earlier, towards the close of his commentary 0 the
Laghumanasa, in the words: “The works written by me earlier are
enumerated (here): First was commented the commentary Vivarana
by Govinda-svami on Bhaskaracarya’s Mahatantra (Mahabhaskariya).”
