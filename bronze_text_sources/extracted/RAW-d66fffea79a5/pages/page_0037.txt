14, Use of ‘Siva’, a unique measure denomination

Under Ganita 26, Stryadeva sets the following example :

“(A certain commodity measuring) one and one-eighth of a Siva
is bought for 124 panas; how much of the same (commodity)

could be bought for 6} panas ?”

The solution given by him shows that the siva mentioned in the

above example is a measure of weight, equivalent to 8 prasthas. In

“Indian mathematical parlance the word Siva is used to denote the

number 11, in conformity with the number of the rudra-s (Siva), being

eleven, but we have so far not come across this term used to indicate

a measure of weight. But use of this measure by Suryadeva shows
that this measure was in use in the locality where Suryadeva lived.
