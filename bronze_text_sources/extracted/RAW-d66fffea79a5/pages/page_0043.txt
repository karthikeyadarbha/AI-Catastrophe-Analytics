10. Mahayatra-Vyakbya

Another work of Stiryadeva about which he makes a mention
towards the end of his commentary on the Laghumanasa, is a
commentary on the Mahayatra of Varahamihira:’ tato Varahamihira-
krta Mahayatra sanksepato vyakhyata. Manuscripts of this work, too,
are yet to be traced. Highly informed as Stryadeva was in ancient
literature on natural astrology, his commentary on the Mahdyatra
should have contained substantial information and citations to earlier
literature on the subject.

11. Khandakhadyaka-vyakhya

Suryadeva states towards the end of his commentary on the
Jatakapaddhati of Stipati that he intended to write a commentary on
the Khangakhadyaka of Brahmagupta : Cf. idanim Bhatta-Brahmagupta-
krta-Khandakhadyaka-vyacikirsd vartate | tam ca Krsno Devakiputrah
phrayisyati. Possibly, this commentary has been written, but manu-
scripts thereof yet remain to be traced.

12. SURYADEVA'S METHOD OF EXPOSITION
1. Presentation of the background

It is noteworthy that Suryadeva always sets out the background
or the intent of a verse before he gives its exposition. In the case
of important sections of the works, he prefixes his commentary with
full-fledged ‘Introductions’ (upedghdta-s) setting out the preliminary
matter for a proper understanding of the respective sections. Such
Introductions occur to the Gola-pada of the Aryabhatiya and sections
relating to the Dhruya-sttras, Sphuta and Triprasna of the Laghumanasa.
