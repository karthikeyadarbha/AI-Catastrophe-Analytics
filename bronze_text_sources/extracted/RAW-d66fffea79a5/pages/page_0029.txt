5. WORKS OF SURYADEVA

1. Known works
Suryadeva was primarily a commentator both on astronomy and
astrology. No original work of his has been unearthed. His known

works are :
Com. on the Aryabhatiya of Aryabhata (b. A. D. 476)
Com, on the Laghumanasa of Muiijala (A. 12. 932)
