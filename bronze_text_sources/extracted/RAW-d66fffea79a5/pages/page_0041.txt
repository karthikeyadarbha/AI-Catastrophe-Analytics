8. Jatakapaddhati-vyakhya (Jatakalankara)

The Jataka-paddhati, called also Jataka-karma-paddhati, in eight
chapters, by Sripati (A.D. 1039), son of Nagadeva and grandson of
Kegava of the Kasyapa gotra, is a popular work of all-India prevalence
on predictive astrology based on horoscopes. Of the nearly 20 known
commentaries on the work,” the Jatakalankara of Stiryadeva is the most
popular, especially in South 17412.

The work commences with an invocation on Stiryadeva’s favourite
deity Lord Krsna :*
