2. Critical approach

Suryadeva’s commentaries are characterised by a critical
approach. This is best exemplified by his commentary on the
Laghumdanasa. Often he distinguishes correct readings from corrupt
ones and, at places, argues about the authenticity of a reading. An
instance in point is his commentary on A, Gola., 14 (ced) :

उज्जयिनी लड्भापाः तच्चतुरंशं समोत्तरतः ।

After explaining the text according to the above reading, SUryadeva
notices that the expression taccaturamée, i.e., one fourth of 90° (= 22}°)
does not have any astronomical significance and, 80, asserts that the
correct reading should be paficadasamse, i.e., one fifteenth of 360°
(=24°) which indicates the place where the Sun has the maximum
declination.’
