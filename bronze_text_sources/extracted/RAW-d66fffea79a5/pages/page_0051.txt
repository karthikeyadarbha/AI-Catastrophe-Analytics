6. Weights and measures

Since a knowledge of the units of measures and weights is
essential for mathematical and astronomical computations and since
_ Stryadeva does not supply this need, Yallaya attempts to give the same
in detail at the commencement of the Kélakriya-pada. The units
- depicted by Yallaya relate to length, grain measure, weight, time and
numerical notation upto 29 places, This is followed by a statement of
the Purdnic legend on the promulgation of numerical notation and

mathematical operation during a conversation between Lord Siva and
Goddess Parvati.
