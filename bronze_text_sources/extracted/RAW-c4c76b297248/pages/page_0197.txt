e Tantric Definition of Birth Time

Tantra has no confusion as to the precise definition of birth time. A Tantric or astrological
birth is marked by the first cry of the child with which the ‘new being’ reports to the
‘eternal being’ Mahakala. More than giving a precise definition, Tantra profess a
mathematically conceivable pattern of destiny operating over the terrestrial existence
based on the longitude of Moon as well as the ascendant or Lagna.
