(v) Attempt to derive justification from Vedanga Jydtisa

Towards the end Lahiri has tried to consecrate the Citrapaksa Zodiac using false
evidence maneuvered in the name of Vedanga Jydtisa. To meet his ends the relevant
verse is interpreted as — ‘winter solstice used to take place...when both the sun and
moon became conjoined with the principal star of this asterism (Dhanistha)’. Whereas the
verse makes no mention of the principal star at all and moreover the principal star is of
latitude 30° and as such it was impossible for sun and moon to conjoin them. Verse only
means that the new moon conjoined solstice at the beginning of the naksatra division
Dhanistha.
