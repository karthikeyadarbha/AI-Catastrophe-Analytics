Ophiucus, Serpens and Hercules

Against Scorpio and Sagittarius on the north of the ecliptic are the big constellations
Ophiucus and Hercules. These constellations too have played a significant role in the
epic war story as great warriors but we are short of details as to identify the specific
characters with either of them.
