9. Year of the Bharata War

Against the above background the problem of dating the war becomes exclusively
astronomical. But despite the availability of modern software, the problem of locating the
eclipses are fraught with the danger when we go back into antiquity. Due to changes in
the speed of earth’s rotation, computations of 1000 BC or beyond may be that accurate
as it should be. Moreover there are chances of duplication i.e., there might be more than
one epoch answering to the above epochal features. Under such circumstances how can
the veracity of a particular answer be ascertained? We have to look for other
substantiating features in the Epic. This is the approach | have taken in fixing the date of
the war.
