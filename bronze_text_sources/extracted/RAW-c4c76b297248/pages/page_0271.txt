5. Sivaratri

Sivaratri is celebrated on the 14" of dark half of Magha or the sidereal solar month of
Kumbha. The original epoch in commemoration of which the festival is celebrated
occurred in 4136 BC and the phenomenon recurs in every 160 years. Last time when the
Mahagivaratri coincided with the end-point of Kumbha is 13/14" March 1945. Originally it
was the winter solstice day that coincided with the boundary of Kumbha rasi but since
then the anniversary of the epoch is under celebration by sidereal reckoning.
