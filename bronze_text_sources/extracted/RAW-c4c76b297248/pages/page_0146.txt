Dikshit has further quoted Taittirilyabrahmana and other Sitras to illustrate the use of the
term ‘astaka’ to designate the 8" night of the dark half. These instances clearly suggest
the reckoning of [चाचा phases in a unit that counted 30 in number - whether these ‘30’
were tithis or not is a question of interpretation. The fact that the use of the term ‘tithi’ is
absent in the Vedic literature is not a sufficient justification for denying the existence of
‘tithi’ in the Vedic civilization. Vedas and Vedic literature cannot be expected to provide an
exhaustive account of all the aspects of the civilization that produced it. ‘Tithi’ being a
technical term had only very little chance to appear in the Vedic hymns and other
associated literature. Normally, we can expect it to appear only in their treatise on
astronomy and the term does appear in Vedaiga Jyotisa in its astronomical form. But
now we are beset with other doubts such as the date of the Vedafiga Jyotisa and whether
the use of tithi can be traced to a period anterior to the extant version of Vedafiga Jyotisa
etc.
