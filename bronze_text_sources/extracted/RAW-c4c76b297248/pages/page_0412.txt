It is apparent from the above that the Dvapara — Kali sandhi associated with the Bharata
War cannot be identified on the basis of Puranic Yuga scheme. On the other hand the
astronomical data of the Mahabharata viz., the two eclipses visible at Kuruksetra, the new
moon and War beginning on Abhijit at 68 days (exactly) prior to the winter solstice,
allegorical characterization of Bhisma, Drona, ASvatthama etc., all point towards 4036 BC
as the main epoch under reference in the War episode. Krsna epoch at 4136 BC and the
respective planetary positions provide indirect substantiation to the interpretation
attempted in this paper.

15. Planetary Astronomy in Pre-historic Times

Present author is well aware of the difficulties in accepting that the planetary astronomy
existed in India in pre-historic times such as say 2000 or 4000 BC. As such to drive the
point more forcefully | would like to quote another remarkable record of ancient planetary
positions from the work of Daftari

Vayupurana Chapter 53:

Vivsvanaditeh putrah stryovaicaksuse tare |
