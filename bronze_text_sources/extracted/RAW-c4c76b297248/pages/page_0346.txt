° Evidences of the Cultural contacts with Mesopotamia
1. Vedic Gods in Mesopotamia

Cambridge ‘History of India’ Vol.1 refers to the discovery of an inscription of Boghoz Koi,
which gives the peace treaty between the king of Hittites and the king of Mitani that took
place around 1400 BC in Asia Minor. This treaty contained the names of the Vedic deities
Mitra, Varuna, Indra and Nasatyas as the Mitani gods. H.Jacobi has expressed the
following views on this discovery:

precisely as we find them grouped in the Veda. In my opinion, this fact establishes the

therefore quite clearly that in the fourteenth century BC and earlier the rulers of Northern

4. Worship of the mountain god and goddess, Mother Goddess etc., also had an
astronomical origin at the above Tantric epoch. As explained before the Phallus or more

mountain’s daughter) who in turn took birth as a “Matsya-Kanya’” in the Matsya country.
