(b) Months were Lunar and Year Solar

Moon offered a visible unit of time in the tunation and thus the term ‘masa’ which was
originally a synonym for the moon was later applied to denote the time unit of a lunation
or month. No such convenient measure as the lunation is available for fixing a solar
month.
