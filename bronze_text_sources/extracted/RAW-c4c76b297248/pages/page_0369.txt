XII

ASTRONOMICAL INTERPRETATION
OF MYTHOLOGY

The script of one of the oldest civilizations that strived in Indus Valley remains
largely enigmatic. As is known the most ancient literature available in the
subcontinent makes no direct mention of these ancient sites. Harappa and
Mohenjo-Daro were discovered in the 1920s and since then more than thousand
other sites have been discovered between Narmada in the south and Oxus in
Central Asia. Since the collapse of the civilization around 1900 BC, there is no
evidence of a script until] about 400 BC in India. Therefore, the dawn of not only
the most ancient civilization of the Indus Valley but also that of the Gangetic plain
remains shrouded in mystery.
