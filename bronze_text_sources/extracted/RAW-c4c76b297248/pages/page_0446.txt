of his terrestrial existence had been very little and the mainstream historians remain
reluctant to award him a historical status. Vikramaditya, thus remains 8 puzzle to all. As
popularly known by legends Vikram equally distributed his life between the kingdom and
the forests by six months each alternatively very much like the solar courses, Uttarayana
