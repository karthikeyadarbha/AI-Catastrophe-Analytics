This identification of Yajfa and Agni as the seasonal year and its subtle beginning meet
with a lot of ramifications in the comprehension of Vedic religion. In our discussions on
Vedic astronomy we have already seen that the hymns themselves formed a part of the
intellectual efforts to achieve a perfect solar calendar by means of appropriate
intercalations. In the work of Daftari we saw that hymns were created at the end of
Yugas/ Manvantaras/ Kalpas of 4-Years and the metre Rathantarm signified the end of a
period of 28 Kalpas. Perhaps a count of the days or years is incorporated in the
metrics/syllable structure of the hymns or we may go a step further and infer that the
chandas was created for the purpose of reigning time. Roy has given an interpretation
slightly different from the common genre to the verse Rgveda 1.164.39 as:

“The rks are written in the high heavens: What shall he do with the earthly rks, he who
cannot read those heavenly ones?”
