15” Mahayuga. The sixth incarnation Jamadagnya Rama was born in Treta of the 1 90
Mahayuga while the seventh incarnation Dasarathi Rama was born in the Treta of the
24" Mahayuga. The eighth incarnation Veda Vyasa, son of Paragara was born in the
Dvapara of the 28" Mahayuga’”.
