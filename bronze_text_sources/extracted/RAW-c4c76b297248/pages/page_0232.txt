e Associated Puranic Legends
(1) Legend of Nahisa

Nahisa’s reign as Indra and his ride over the Seven-sages to meet Indrani is another
related astro-mythological legend. Nahisa uttered ‘sarpa’, ‘sarpa’ to speed up the Sages
and received the curse of Angiras to become a Sarpa or serpent (Dragon). This legend in
fact refers to the reign of one of the stars of Draco as the Polestar and its subsequent fall.
Reference to Nahisa can be found in the Rgveda itself and is reflective of the antiquity of
the astronomical observations by the Vedic Rsis. Note the cryptic astronomical clue
‘Sarpa’ that appears in the allegory to stand for the Draco.
