eMilky Way is the galaxy of which our solar system forms a part and its centre is
located near 2° Sagittarius. The starry band that gives the impression of a milky
ocean extends between 25° Taurus and 10° Cancer to 12° Scorpio and 21°
Sagittarius. In reality, it is a huge wheel like structure hundred thousand light
years in diameter and ten thousand light-years in thickness.
