ASTRONOMICAL BASIS
OF HINDU RELIGION

“No system, no theory, no formula, connecting those phenomena could possibly
have sprung suddenly into existence at the call, or upon the dictation of a single
genius. Far rather it is to be supposed that little by little, and after many arduous
labours of numerous minds, and many consequent periods passed in the
investigation of isolated phenomena, a system could be expected to be formed
into a general science concerning them”.

W. Brennand
