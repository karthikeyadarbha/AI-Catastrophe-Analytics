The unbreakable bow of Siva is of course the sign of Dhanu ~ Sagittarius. A legend in the
Kathak Samhita states that the Gods had created an unbreakable bow. But only Rudra
the most powerful amongst them could wield and use it to vanquish the enemy. They
therefore made him the Lord of the ‘animal world’ for seven days and urged on him to
take up the bow. Mark well — the mention of ‘Seven days’ in this place, which convincingly
establishes that at the time when famous Rudra hymn was sung, the ‘seven days’ week
and the luminaries after which they were named had come in vogue in everyday life’.
