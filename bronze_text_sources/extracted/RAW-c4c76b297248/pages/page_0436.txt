“Last of all, it may be urged that Bhisma 35 hero in the great fight is an impossibility -

° If suppose such a war has really taken place in history and the astronomical allegory of

e Vedafga Jyotisa is generally looked upon as the climax of pre-Siddhantic Indian

indicate that the astronomical data implicit in the allegorical description of the Pusya
