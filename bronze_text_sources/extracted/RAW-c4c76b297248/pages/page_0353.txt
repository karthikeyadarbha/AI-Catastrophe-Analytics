Translation of verses 104 — 109:

e 104:"Vivasvan, the son of Aditi, the first among the planets, i.e., the Sun-god, was born
under the constellation ViSakha in the Caksusa Manvantara’”.

e 105: “The brilliant Moon-god Vi$vavasu, the son of Dharma, the cool-rayed maker of
nights, was born under (the constellation) Krttika”.

e 106: “After the Sun, Sukra (Venus), son of Bhrgu, endowed with sixteen rays, who is
next to the Sun is the most excellent among constellations and planets. He is born in the
constellation Tisya (Pusya)/ Nistya (Svati)”.

° 107:’The planet Brhaspati (Jupiter) of twelve fiery flames, who is the son of Afigiras and
the preceptor of the Universe was born under the constellation Phalguni.

e 108: “It is reported that the nine-rayed, red-bodied planet (Mars), son of Prajapati, is
born under the constellation Pirvasadha’”.

e 109:” The seven rayed planet, Saturn, son of the Sun, is born under the constellation

following naksatras: Sun: Visakha, Moon: Krttika, Venus: Tisya? (Perhaps Nistya),
Jupiter: Phalguni, Mars: Purvasadha, Saturn: Revati and Rahu: Revati. Daftari could
