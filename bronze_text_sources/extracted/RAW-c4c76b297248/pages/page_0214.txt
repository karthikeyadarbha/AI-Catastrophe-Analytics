literature. Taittirlya Brahmana (2.7.10) has spoken of the five “Saradiyas” as seasons of
the year. Aitareya Brahmana (1.1) also has such a mention where Hemanta and 51518

e Taittirntya Samhita 6.5.3: “Two faced is the vessel of seasons; who knows which one is

eTaittinya Brahmana 3.10.1 declares Samvatsara as the Prajapati. Satapathabrahmana
(1.6.3) contains the following elaboration:

“After mankind was created by Prajapati, his parvas (knuckles) became loose. The
samvatsara (year) itself represented the Prajapati. The two points of day and night i.e.
twilight, the full moon and the new moon and the commencement of seasons — these are
his parvas. The gods diagnosed the disorder. They cured the joints’ of day and night and
set them in their correct places by maintenance of agnihotras (sacred fires). The full and
the new moons were correctly adjusted by the Pournamaseshti and Darseshti and the
joints of seasons were set right by means of the ‘Caturmasyayajna’ (i.e., four monthly
sacrifices)”. ।
