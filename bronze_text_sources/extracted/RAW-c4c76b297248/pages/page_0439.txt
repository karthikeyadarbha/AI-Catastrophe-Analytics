3. Date of Adi Sankaracarya
* Gururatnamalika gives Sankara’s date as:

Tisye prayatyanalaSevatibananetre fode nandanedinamanavudagdhvabhaji |
Radhefditeruduvinirgatamastralagnefsyahitavan Sivaguruh sa ca Sarikareti ||

According to this verse Safkara was born in Dhanur lagna and Punarvasu naksatra, in
the month of Vaisakha during Uttarayana when 2593 years of Kaliyuga had elapsed,
which corresponds to BC 508. Scholars have deciphered other details such as
Suklapancami and sunday also from this verse and have considered this as very
authentic information quoted by Atmabddha, 58" Acarya of Kamakéti Pitha in his
commentary Susama on the Gururatnamalika.

Jinavijaya, a Jaina work likewise gives the date of Kuméarila Bhatta as the year 2077 of
the Yudhistira era that corresponds to 557 BC. Same work gives Sarikara’s demise in the
year 2157 Yudhistira era or 477 BC.

Brhat Safkaravijaya of Citsukac@rya gives the detailed horoscope itself for the date
falling in 2631 of Yudhistira era [508 BC] and accordingly the lagna is Kataka and the
planets Sun, Venus, Jupiter, Mars and Saturn were in exaltation signs. Authors A.
Nataraja lyer and 5. Lakshminarasimha 588} who produced the work The Traditional

Citsukacarya is described as the lifelong companion and disciple of Sankara and as such
