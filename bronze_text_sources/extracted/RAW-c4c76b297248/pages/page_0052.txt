(b) Beginning of Kaliyuga

No description of Indian chronology is possible without referring to the beginning of
Kaliyuga. In terms of popular records, Aryabhata, who lived in the sixth century AD,
perhaps spoke for the first time of an antiquity of 3600 years for the beginning of Kaliyuga
and the Bharata war. Aihole inscription of Pulakesin ॥ referred to 3735 years of Kaliyuga
that coincided with the year 556 of the Saka era and rendered archaeological support to
the tradition reflected in Aryabhatiyam. Much of the Puranic chronology tends to have an
inherent dependence upon the Aryabhata tradition that places Kaliyugadi in 3102 BC.
The chronology deciphered out of the Bhagavatapurana by William Jones in January
1788, also had its beginning with the Kaliyugadi of 3102 BC.
