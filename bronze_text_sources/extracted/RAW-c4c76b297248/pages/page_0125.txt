Development Profile and Personages of Tantric Tradition

As | have mentioned above we have no source of a reliable chronology of Indian antiquity
and all that we have as chronology are guess works based on confusing Puranic
accounts. Thus going by factual information we are not in a position to ascribe any
precise dates to the various stages of developments or personages who have enriched
the Tantric tradition. But still the profile that Svami Sakhyananda has drawn of the
development of the discipline is worth taking a notice. The salient points are:

e Yoga — Vedanta discipline had its origin from Sadasiva and at some stage (perhaps
inspired by sociological factors or may be due to intellectual conflicts upon modes of
practices) the Acarya — parampara split into two: Agama and Nigama presided
respectively by the Rajarsis and Brahmarsis.

e Nigamikas worshipped the ‘Nada-brahmam’ and under the precepts of Brahmarsis
developed the ‘Vedavanl’ rooted in Chandas and in due course claimed supremacy
over the Agamikas.

e Agamikas continued the silent pursuit of Yoga and developed the Tantragama
Sastras under Rajarsis like Visvamitra, Janaka etc.
