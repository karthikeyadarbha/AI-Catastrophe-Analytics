Candrasekhara, Tarunendusekhara (bearer of the crescent Moon) etc. also arose out of
the synonymy with Time. ‘Kalanatmaka iti Kalah' - We must remember that ail the
divisions of time computation such as Rudra-dina, Visnu-dina, Brahma-dina, Kalpa,
Manvantara , Mahayuga, Samvatsara and Masah begin with Sukla-pratipada - the first
tithi of the bright half of Moon's synodical revolution. Vedas hence describe Candra as the
Masakrd or the creator of months. The crescent Moon over Rudra’s head thus signifies
the epochal beginning of Time.
