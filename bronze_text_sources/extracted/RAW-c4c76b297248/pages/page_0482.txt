to the horizon and attains its maximum altitude in the south while in the north equator

° Ecliptic and Equinoctial points

is also spinning like a gyroscope, keeping its spin axis in a fixed orientation in space
through out its annual motion round the sun. As a result sun appears to slowly move
against the backdrop of stars round the celestial sphere during the course of a year and
this apparent annual path of the sun is called the ‘Ecliptic
