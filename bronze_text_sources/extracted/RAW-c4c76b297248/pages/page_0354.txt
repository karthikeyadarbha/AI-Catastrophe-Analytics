noted that even the position of Rahu (stated as in Revati but actually it is in U.Bhadra)
could be ascertained in 2422 BC with considerable accuracy. The position of Venus is not
that clear from the verse and we may take it as Nistya the synonym of Svati instead of
Tisya. Another remarkable aspect is that moon had a northern latitude of 04°06’ and rose
on that day in rapt conjunction with Kritika (Alcyone: 35°24’, Latitude:04°03’) at 18:19 IST
on 30° N with the longitude of 35°54’ and latitude 04°14’. It is therefore apparent that the
rising moon had occulted Alcyone (n-Tauri) and the epochal observation was recorded
meticulously along with the positions of other planets. ।
