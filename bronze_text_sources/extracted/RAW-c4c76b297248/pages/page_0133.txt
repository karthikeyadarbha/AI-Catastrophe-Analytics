Legend of Hari-Hara Putra

Recovery of the amrtam (or Calendar) by Devas with the help of Visnu as Mohini and the
birth of a son for Rudra and Mohini in Uttaraphadiguni naksatra also refers to the year-
beginning at summer solstice falling at 150° of the Sidereal zodiac. Visnu as MGhini is the
sun entering Kanya Rasi and Rudra is Mila as before. Hari or Visnu is also the Simha
Rasi and hence the solstice falling over 150° became Hari-Hara Putra in mythology
Moreover this deity is Pulivahana i.e., rides over Lion (cryptic reference to the sign Leo)
and Brahmacari - averse to women - as the solstice has already moved over the Kanya
Rasi from east to west and also Kanya being mother as MShini from a different angle.

Astronomy underlying the legend of Hari-Hara Putra finds a beautiful demonstration in the
Sabrimala Sri Dharma Sastha Temple of Kerala. In clear-cut symbolism with the
11126118 Cakra, the deity is Yogaroodha (in Yogic posture) and sits over 18 holy steps
that signify the 18 steps of Yogavidya as well as the ascent of Kundalini from
Miladharam (240°) to Sirsa-padmam (0°) over the cerebro-spinal axis. These 18 steps
are indicative of the fiducial star located at the end of the 18 stellar divisions i.e., 18 x
13°20' = 240°. Further it is customary that the devotees must carry with them a coconut
(three-eyed) as representing the Trayambaka-Rudra. The first visit to the shrine receives
the appellation Kanni (Malayalam term for Kanya). This is reflective of the year beginning
with Kanya Rasi and the tradition prevails even now in Malabar (North Kerala). Legends
depict the avtar of Sastha as an Archer riding over lion (Indra assumed the form of a lion
along with other Devas) who shoots an arrow to the Sabari hills to pinpoint the location of
his divine presence. This is reflective of the Rasis Dhanu and Simha and the Orion’s belt
