Though numerous manuals, both in Malayalam and in Sanskrit,
following the Parahita system are known and used, the basic work of the
system was unknown till recently when the present editor could discover
it and edit it on the basis of five manuscripts of the work.®
Advent of Drgganita

The necessity of revising the Parahita system became apparent
some centuries later, when, due to the accumulation of minute errors in
the fundamentals during the course of years, the results of computation
began to differ appreciably from those of actual observation. It was
this that prompted our author in his investigations which resulted in the
enunciation of his revised Drk system. This is mentioned by him in the
beginning of his Drgganita :
