The Indian Institute of Advanced Study deems it a privilege to have taken up
the three-volume project of Ganita- yukti-bhasé through the critical edition of its
Malayalam and Sanskrit versions and an English Translation of the work along
with detailed explanatory notes. It would seem, as indicated earlier, that there
has been no other text in Indian astronomical literature produced in India or
elsewhere which is as full and elucidative as Ganita-yukti-bhasa in expounding
Indian mathematical and astronomical rationale. We have great pleasure in placing
the said three volumes before the scholarly public for their edification.

The credit for completing the work and making it accessible to modern readers
goes entirely to Professor K. V. Sarma, whose renown in this field is too well-
known to need elaboration, and his colleagues Dr. K. Ramasubramanian, Prof.
M.S. Sriram and Prof. M.D. Srinivas. The Institute is deeply beholden to them for
this monumental work.
