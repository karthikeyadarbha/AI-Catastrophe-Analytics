the rule, and so is to be welcomed with greater interest, for that helps us in
reconstructing the working of the mind of the early and medieval Indian
mathematician and astronomer.

6. The case of in Kerala

It is well known that from the 7th century onwards, if not earlier, Kerala
has remained the bastion of the Aryabhatan School of astronomy. There the
discipline flourished under royal patronage and was assiduously followed by
the Namputiri brahmins of Kerala. It is interesting that among the works
composed here, alongside other texts, a number of texts on mathematical and
astronomical rationale, belonging to all the categories, also came to be
composed. Hereinbelow, a mention is made of the rich crop of literature on
rationale produced in Kerala. Most of the texts are anonymous, but the dates
of several of them can be ascertained from the epoch or cut off dates
mentioned in these, or from other indications. In this set up, it is to be hoped
that, besides being informative and instructive in the matter of probing the
mental make-up, intellectual interests and assiduity of the mathematicians and
astronomers of the land, especially during the medieval times, the present
work would encourage the texts being culled out individually or in groups for
analytical study and interpretation in terms of modern mathematics, and,
eventually, for a better appraisal of the development of mathematical ideas
and procedures in the land.
