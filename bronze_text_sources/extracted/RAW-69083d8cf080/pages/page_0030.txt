9. Innovative Texts

A number of full-fledged texts based on earlier Siddhantas, but
incorporating and involving major revisions, innovations, and methodologies,
peculiar to Kerala have been produced down the ages. These are of two types,
viz., those which take up for treatment the entire range of the subject, and
those which take up only one or more topics therein.
