Index of Technical Terms: Sanskrit - English

साङ्केतिकपदसूची - संस्कृतम्‌-आंगलभाषा च

The references suffixed to the terms are to the numbers of
chapters and sections of their place of occurrence.

Since most of the terms occur frequently all through the
volume references are given only to the places of their first
occurrence where mostly their definitions are also given.

‘Common words' (c.w.) used in their general sense, may be
with a technical twist and, so, do not have any specific
definition in the text are given with the abbreviation ‘cw’
suffixed to them, but with no textual references.

A technical term 'derived' from another technical term and, so,
is not given special definitions in the text, are marked with the
abbreviation 'd.w.' and included in the Index with no textual
reference.
