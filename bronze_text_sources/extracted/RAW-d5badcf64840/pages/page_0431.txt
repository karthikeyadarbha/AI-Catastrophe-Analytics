      The [period of the] three vyatı̄pātas (lāt.a, vaidhr.ta and sārpa-mastaka) is [considered
      to be] dreadful and is inauspicious for performing all religious rites. But by acquiring the
      correct knowledge of these periods and performing certain deeds such as having a holy dip,
      performing charitable deeds or sacrificial deeds, doing penance, oath-taking, performing
      homa etc. one reaps great benefits.
