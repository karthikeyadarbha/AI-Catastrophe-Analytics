   Having added the kot.iphala to the radius (vyāsārdha) in the first and the fourth quadrants
   and having subtracted [the kot.iphala] from it (the radius) in the other two [quadrants] let
   the square root of the sum of the squares of this and the doh.phala be obtained. This is the
   karn. a and in the manda process this has to be further iterated upon, but not in the śı̄ghra
   (cala).
