Vyatı̄pāta

We have a vyatı̄pāta when the magnitudes of the declinations of the Sun and the
Moon are equal and their gradients are in opposite directions. This concept seems
to be peculiar to Indian astronomy. Issues such as the occurrence/non-occurrence of
vyatı̄pāta, its duration etc. are discussed in detail in this chapter.
