sva (one’s own) with the word paryaya (revolution). This special usage is com-

      The circles of Mercury and Venus (the actual orbits in which they move) become the
      śaighrya-nı̄cocca-vr. tta [in the śighra-sam . skāra]. The fact that the numbers of revo-
      lutions given here are the revolutions of the Mercury and Venus, moving in these circles
      with their own velocities, is indicated by the word sva.

   In fact, this novel view regarding the motion of interior planets—according to
which what were traditionally identified as the śighroccas are now identified with
the mean planets themselves—became the starting point of a major revision of the
traditional planetary model. This will be explained in Chapter 2 as well as in Ap-
pendix F.
