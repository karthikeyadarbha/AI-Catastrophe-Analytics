Significance of the condition that δ < φ and it must be north

1. If the condition δ < φ is not satisfied, then the Sun will never cross the prime ver-
   tical during its diurnal motion, and hence the expression for the samaman. .dala-
   śaṅku (3.129) does not have any significance.
2. If the Sun does not have a northern declination, then it will not be above the
   horizon when it crosses the prime vertical.
