      The longitude of the planet found for tomorrow is subtracted from the longitude of the planet
      today. The result [if positive] is the retrograde daily motion of the planet; if otherwise, the

   In this verse, essentially, the definition of direct/retrograde motion is given. By
bhoga is meant daily motion, the angular distance travelled by the planet in one day
as observed by an observer on the surface of the Earth.
