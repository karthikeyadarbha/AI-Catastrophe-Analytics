   As the axis of rotation of the Earth is perpendicular to the equator, the rotation
angle measured along the equator is related to time and can be expressed in prān.as.
One prān.a corresponds to one minute of arc along the equator. Since the right as-
cension is an arc measured along the equator, α is expressed in prān.as.
