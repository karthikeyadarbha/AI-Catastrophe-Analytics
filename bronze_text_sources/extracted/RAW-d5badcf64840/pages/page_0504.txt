The equatorial system

In this system, the celestial equator is taken to be the fundamental plane with ref-
erence to which the coordinates are specified. The celestial equator is a great circle
whose plane is perpendicular to OP. Clearly, its plane is parallel to that of the Earth’s
equator. This would be inclined to the horizon by an angle equal to the co-latitude
(90 − φ ) of the observer (see Fig. C.4).
