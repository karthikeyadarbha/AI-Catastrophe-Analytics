   The sum or difference of the product of the trijyā and the apakrama and that of the śaṅku
   and the aks.a is found, depending upon whether the Sun is in the northern or the southern
   hemisphere. The result divided by the lambaka is the bhābāhu. This multiplied by the
   trijyā and divided by the bhā (chāyā) is the is..tadik (āśāgrā).

    In the sixth problem the hour angle (nata) and amplitude (āśāgrā) are obtained
in terms of the zenith distance (śaṅku), declination (apakrama or krānti) and the
latitude (aks.a). That is, H and a are obtained in terms of z, δ and φ .
    The expression for the krānti is given in two steps. Initially a term called the
bhābāhu is defined thus:
