   It may be noted that the periods of these four yugas are in the ratio 4:3:2:1 re-
spectively. Because of this pattern the total number of years in a Mahāyuga happens
to be just 10 times the number of years in the Kaliyuga. However, some scholars
are of the view that Āryabhat.a might have employed a different scheme for the di-
vision of the four yugas, in which all of them are taken to be of equal periods. This
view is based on the use of the term ‘pāda’, which means quarter, in the expression
‘kalpāderyugapādāh. ga ca’ (... [and] three-fourths of the yuga [have elapsed] since
the beginning of the Kalpa [till the beginning of the current Kaliyuga]).36
