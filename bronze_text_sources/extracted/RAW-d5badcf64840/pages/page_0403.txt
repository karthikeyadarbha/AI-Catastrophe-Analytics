   . . . all that was described earlier is recalled. There, to the line thus drawn parallel to the
   east-west line, as per the direction [determined], another line is to be drawn east-west at a
   distance of its own parallax in latitude from the ecliptic. That is the [instantaneous] path of
   the Sun. Then again draw an east-west line along that direction at a distance equal to the
   difference in the deflection from the ecliptic of the Sun and the Moon. That is the path of
   the Moon.
