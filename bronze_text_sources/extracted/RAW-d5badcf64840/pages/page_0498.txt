Analogue of the cosine formula

One more formula, involving the three sides and two angles, that is often found to
be useful in solving problems is

                          sin a cosB = cosb sin c − sin b cosc cos A.                   (B.12)

This formula generally goes by the name of the ‘analogue of the cosine formula’ as
it is simply obtained by substituting the cosine formula in itself. For instance, the
above formula is obtained by substituting (B.3) in (B.4).
