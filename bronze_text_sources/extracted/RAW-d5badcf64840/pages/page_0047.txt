xlvi                                                                       Introduction

   Nı̄lakan.t.ha takes up the issue of planetary distances in a couple of verses at the
very end of the last chapter. Here he seems to suggest that the kaks.yā-vyāsārdha,
or the mean distances in yojanas, obtained from the principle that all planets cover
equal distances in equal times, should be understood as the mean śı̄ghrocca–planet
distance, and not as the mean Earth–planet distances as comprehended in earlier
texts.
