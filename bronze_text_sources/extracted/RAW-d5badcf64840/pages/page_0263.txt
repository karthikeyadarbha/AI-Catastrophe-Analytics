      The kot.i of the natajyā is multiplied by the dyujyā and divided by the trijyā. Depending
      upon whether the Sun is to the north or south, the bhūjyā is added to or subtracted from this
      and the result, multiplied by the lambaka and divided by the trijyā, becomes the śaṅku.
      The kot.is of the āśāgrā and the dyujyā [are to be obtained] as before.

Here, the zenith distance (śaṅku) and amplitude (āśāgrā) are obtained in terms of
the hour angle (nata), the latitude (aks.a) and the declination (krānti). That is, z and
a are obtained in terms of H, φ and δ .
