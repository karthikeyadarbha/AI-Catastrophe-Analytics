562                                                                 Glossary

yuga           Aeon; a large unit of time, for instance, Kaliyuga whose
               duration is 432000 years or Mahāyuga made of 4320000
               years; could also refer to a short unit like 576 years as in
               Tantrasaṅgraha.
yugabhagan.a   Number of revolutions made by a planet in the course of a
               Mahāyuga (4320000 years).
yugma          (1) Even. (2) The second and fourth quadrants in a circle.
yukti          Proof; rationale; reasoned justification.
