      Of the two points whose jyā values are listed in the table, find the one which is closer to the
      desired jyā [whose arc value is to be found]. Then find the difference between these two
      jyās (dorjyās), and divide the sum of the kot.ijyās by this difference. The result is called
      the cheda. Divide twice the trijyā by this cheda. The result obtained is the difference
      between the arcs lying between the desired jyā, and the jyā closest to it (as found from the
      table); that is, it gives the length of the arc corresponding to the difference in the dorjyās.
