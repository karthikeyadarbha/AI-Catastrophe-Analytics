    through the zenith of the observer]. The one which is exactly perpendicular [to that] is the
    prime meridian (daks.in . ottara-man  . .dala). The [horizontal plane] around the centre of the
    solid Earth would be the horizon for places with zero latitude. The intersection of the north–
    south circle and this (horizon) will always be the north and south celestial poles (dhruvas).
