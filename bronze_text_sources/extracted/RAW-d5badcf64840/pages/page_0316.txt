   The Rsine of the longitude of the node subtracted from the Moon is multiplied by 270 and
   divided by the trijyā. This gives the latitude of the Moon lying to the north or south [of the
   ecliptic]. This and the true daily motion, multiplied by the bhagola-candra-karn       . a, and
   divided by the actual distance of separation between the Earth and the Moon in yojanas
   (d2m ), are the true values of the latitude and the daily motion at the middle of the eclipse,
   which are to be considered [for computational purposes].
