Fig. 1.1 The diurnal motion of a celestial object.


    As the Earth rotates around its axis from west to east, the apparent motion of the
celestial objects is from east to west (see Fig. 1.1). This apparent westward motion
of the objects as seen by the terrestrial observer is described as pratyagbhrama.
The word pratyak means west, and bhrama is motion, and hence pratyagbhrama
is westward motion. In modern spherical astronomy, this apparent westward motion
is termed the ‘diurnal motion’.
