                                          p

In the above relation, OA is the difference in the longitude of the chāyā and the
Moon (∆ λ ′ ), and AX is the true latitude of the Moon (βt ). Though the values of
both are known, the above relation is an approximate one because OA and AX are
segments of great circles and not parts of a planar triangle.

mine OX . From X, XQ = R sin βt , is drawn perpendicular to the plane of the ecliptic,
where βt is the latitude. As OQ is in the plane of ecliptic, OQX forms a right-angled
