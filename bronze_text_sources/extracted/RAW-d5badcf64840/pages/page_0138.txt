   The arc corresponding to a jyā may be obtained either by finding the difference between
   the jyā and the arc as given in the verse [beginning] śis..tacāpaghana etc., and adding that
   (difference) to the jyā, or from the table of jyās listed earlier.
   The square of the trijyā is 11818103 (in minutes). Multiply this by 1, 2 etc., divide by 10
   and find the cube roots of these results. If the jyā (whose arc is to be found) has a measure
   equal to these (the above cube roots), then 1, 2, etc. seconds have to be added to them. Thus
   the arc of the R sine of small angles involved in the caradoh.phala may be obtained.
