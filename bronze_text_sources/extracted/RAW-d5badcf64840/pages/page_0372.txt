Case (iii): The first contact, the middle and the last contact in different hemispheres
   A typical case of the first contact and the middle happening in different hemi-
spheres is shown in Fig. 5.12. Here the expression for the half-duration is given
by

                               ∆ t1′ = S1′ S2′
                                     = S1′ S2 + S2′ S2
                                     = S1′ S1 + S1 S2 + S2′ S2
                                     = S1 S2 + (S1′ S1 + S2′ S2 )
                                     = ∆ t1 + (l1 + l2 ).                                       (5.49)

Similarly it can be shown that if the middle and the last contact happen in different
hemispheres, then the expression for the half-duration will be

                               ∆ t2′ = S2′ S3′
                                     = S2 S3 + (S2′ S2 + S3′ S3 )
                                     = ∆ t2 + (l2 + l3 ).                                       (5.50)
