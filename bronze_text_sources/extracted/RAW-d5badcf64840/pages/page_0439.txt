    Earlier in Chapter 2, we have discussed the revision of the planetary model by
Nı̄lakan.t.ha, and his geometrical model of planetary motion. In this unified model
for both exterior and interior planets, each planet moves in an eccentric circle around
the śı̄ghrocca, which is the mean Sun. The longitude of the planet on the eccentric
orbit with respect to the mean Sun corresponds to the manda-sphut.a-graha, which
is the true heliocentric longitude. The mean Sun itself moves around the Earth uni-
formly in the plane of the ecliptic. Taking this into account, we obtain a longitude
of the planet with respect to the Earth, which is the geocentric longitude. The same
considerations apply for a unified model of latitudes, presented in the above verses.
