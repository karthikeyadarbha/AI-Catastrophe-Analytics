The effective nati

We are actually interested in ne , the effective deflection of the Moon from the Sun,
because that is what determines the duration of a solar eclipse. In all cases, ne = nt −
ns . In the determination of ne , one has to be very careful regarding the directions.
All the possible cases are discussed in the verses of the text.

Case (i): ns and nt have opposite directions
   Here ns has a direction opposite to nt . The magnitude of the effective deflection
from the ecliptic is obtained by finding the sum of the magnitudes of ns and nt as
shown in Fig. 5.7(a). It is given by RA + SB. That is

                                                    |ne | = |nt | + |ns |.                                         (5.36)

The direction of the effective deflection from the ecliptic is the same as that of the
Moon, that is RA.
