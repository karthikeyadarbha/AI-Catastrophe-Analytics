      The Sun, being a ball of effulgence, with a large diameter (mahā-vyāsa), illuminates one
      half of all the objects facing towards him, [thereby] generating a shadow on the other half
      which thins out gradually.
      Hence that half of the Earth facing the Sun is bright and the other half is dark. The shadow
      of that (the Earth’s disc) has a diameter equal to the diameter of the Earth at the beginning
      and gradually becomes thin.
