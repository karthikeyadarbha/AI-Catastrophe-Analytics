   The total duration of the eclipse may be conceived as made up of two parts:
1. the time interval between the instant at which the Moon enters the shadow and
   the instant of opposition (∆ t1 ) and,
2. the time interval between the instants of opposition and complete release (∆ t2 ).
The suffixes 1 and 2 refer to the first and the second half-durations of the eclipse
respectively. Though one may think naively that these two durations must be equal,
this is not so because of the continuous change in the angular velocities of the Sun
and the Moon and its nodes.

the angular velocities of the Sun and the Moon per day, then the difference in their
daily motion called the gatyantara or the bhuktyantara is given by
