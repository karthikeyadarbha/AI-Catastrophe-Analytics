The iteration procedure given in the text may be described as follows: Let λ be the
correct value of the longitude to be found by successive approximations. As a first
approximation we take the value of λ to be the R.A. of T itself. That is,

                                                λ1 = αT .                                (3.322)

The corresponding R.A. is α1 = f (λ1 ). Next, we find the prān.akalāntara (δ α1 ),

                    δ α1 = λ1 − α1 = λ1 − f (λ1 ) = αT − f (αT ).                        (3.323)
