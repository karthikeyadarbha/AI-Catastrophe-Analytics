   Nı̄lakan.t.ha takes up the issue of planetary distances towards the very end of
the last chapter (Chapter 8) of Tantrasaṅgraha. Here, he first notes that the mean
radius of the orbit (kaks.yāvyāsārdha) of each planet is to be found in the same way
as was prescribed in the case of the Sun in Chapter 4, namely by multiplying the
kaks.yāvyāsārdha and the revolutions in a Mahāyuga of the Moon, and dividing the
product by the revolutions of the planet in a Mahāyuga.
