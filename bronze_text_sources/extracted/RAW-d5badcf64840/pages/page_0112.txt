    Having noted down the listed/tabulated values (samı̄rita) of the dorjyās (Rsines) and
    kot.ijyās (Rcosines) corresponding to the two points which are on either side of the arc
    whose dorjyā and kot.ijyā are desired, find the difference in the arc which may be in excess
    of or short of it. [The number] 13751 divided by twice this difference has to be stored [as
    divisor D] for dividing. This is done for mutual correction (i.e. for correcting the dorjyā
    in determining kot.ijyā and vice versa). First divide one of them (the dorjyā or kot.ijyā by
    D) and add or subtract this from the other (if the dorjyā is divided, apply it to the kot.ijyā
    and if the kot.ijyā is divided, apply it to the dorjyā) depending upon whether the difference
    is in excess or short. This result multiplied by two and operated as before (divided by D
    and applied to the dorjyā or kot.ijyā) forms the process of correction. The correction thus
    carried out gives the exact value of the dorjyā or the kot.ijyā of the desired arc. Of the two
    (dorjyā or kot.ijyā) find the square of the jyā of the smaller one and subtract it from the
    square of the trijyā. The square root of the result gives the other (the kot.ijyā or dorjyā).
