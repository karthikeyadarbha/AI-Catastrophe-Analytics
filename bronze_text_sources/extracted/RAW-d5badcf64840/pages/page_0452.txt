      The mean motion of the Moon multiplied by 10 and the trijyā, and [then] divided by the
      distance of separation between the Earth and the Moon in yojanas, is considered to be the
      true motion of the Moon.

a constant, then the product of the true distance (Dm ) and the true daily motion (dm′ )
will be equal to the product of the mean distance (10R) and the mean daily motion
(dm ). That is,
