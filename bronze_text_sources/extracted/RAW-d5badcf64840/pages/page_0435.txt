Āyana correction

In order to find the expression for the āyana-dr.kkarma, δ ′ , which is the declination
of the point on the ecliptic corresponding to λ + 90, i.e. the sāyana longitude of the
planet increased by 90 degrees, is to be determined. Now, the āyana correction x is
given to be
                            x(sec) = β (min) × δ ′ (degrees).                     (7.13)
If λ is the sāyana longitude of the planet, then it is stated that the āyana corrected
longitude is given by
                                     λ ′ = λ ± x,                                 (7.14)
where the sign ‘−’ is to be chosen if δ ′ and β are in the same hemisphere, and ‘+’
is to be chosen if they are in different hemispheres.
