Āryabhat.a on diurnal motion

It is well known that Āryabhat.a was the first Indian astronomer to have suggested
that the apparent diurnal motion of the celestial objects is actually due to the rotation
of the Earth. It is described thus in Āryabhat.ı̄ya:

   Just as a person in a boat moving in the forward direction observes the stationary objects

This verse clearly describes the rotation of the Earth. Āryabhat.a has presented an
apt example to describe relative motion and seems to explain the observed phe-
nomenon of diurnal motion of celestial objects as being actually due to the rotation
of the Earth around its axis.
