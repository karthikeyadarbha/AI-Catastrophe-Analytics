      The earlier ācaryās have stated that manda, śı̄ghra, and again manda and śı̄ghra are
      the four corrections which have to be applied in sequence to the planets Mars, Jupiter and
      Saturn [to obtain the true longitudes of the planets from their mean longitudes].

    Though there are essentially only two corrections, namely manda and śı̄ghra
for the actual planets, that is Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn, the actual
computation of their longitude involves a four-step procedure in most Indian texts.
Nı̄lakan. .tha, as we shall see below, prescribes this four-step process only in the case
of the exterior planets, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn. The actual procedure prescribed in
Tantrasaṅgraha is described in the next few verses.
