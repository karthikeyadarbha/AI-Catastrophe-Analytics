C.4 Equation of time                                                                     465

1. When the Sun is at the perigee, the dynamical mean Sun starts off its motion
   along the ecliptic with a uniform angular speed in such a way that it would meet
   the true Sun again at the perigee after one complete revolution.
2. The motion of the mean Sun is such that it starts off its motion along the
   equator—with uniform angular speed, when the dynamical mean Sun is at the
   vernal equinox—in such a way that it would meet the dynamical mean Sun again
   at the vernal equinox after one complete revolution.
   The difference between the right ascension of the mean Sun (RAMS) and that of
the actual Sun (RA⊙) is defined as the equation of time (ε ) . That is,

                                   ε = RAMS − RA ⊙                                    (C.11)
                                     = H.A. ⊙ −H.A.M.S.                               (C.12)
