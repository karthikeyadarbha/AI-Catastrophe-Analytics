18.2 Jn ancient times, extraordinarily fast and strong winged hills flying
in the sky, falling down in hundreds shook the earth continuously. Having
seen this shaking of the earth, Indra threw his weapon to cut off the wings
of the flying hills. He told Earth, ‘henceforth I will remove your fear caused
by the rocks. But occasionally disturbance caused by anila (vadyu, wind or
gas), anala (agni or fire), ambupati (varuna or oceans) and myself (Indra)
will occur indicating good and bad effects to the world.’ Day and night
each are divided into four parts. The earthquakes are induced in the same
order by anila, anala, indra, varuna respectively. Eclipse of Sun, Moon
and anomalistic movement of planets are also said to induce earthquakes.
