18.7 Events occurring with pleasant sounds that follow in the clockwise
direction during New and Full Moon with heavy rains are beneficial. But
when too many occur at frequent intervals, it portends ta to the king.
Earthquake repeatedly occurring at three, four, seven nights and also at
fortnight, month and one and half month interval, is for the destruction of

an important king. The bad omens indicated by earthquakes get nullified
quickly in seven days by religious rites.
