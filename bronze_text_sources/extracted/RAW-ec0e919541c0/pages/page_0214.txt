17.2 Nucleation or conception of clouds is characterized by wind, lightning,
thunder and precipitation. One has to observe whether these are conducive
to rains in its season. Good portents depend on Sun, Moon and other
atmospheric conditions for rain cloud formation and its holding (for 6
months), without precipitation (miscarriage) in the month of conception.
In whichever stars the cloud conception takes place, if in those naksatras,
malefic planets rise, meteors shower, earth quakes, Suns rays get distorted
there will be no rains in the season. Especially the aimospheric conditions
during the 12" to15" days of the bright fortnight, in the month of Jyestha,
(the first month of summer) are important for nucleation of rain bearing
