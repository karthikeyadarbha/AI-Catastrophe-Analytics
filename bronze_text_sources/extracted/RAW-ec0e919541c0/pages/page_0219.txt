17.7 Rains in the first part of the day are good. In the second half, crops
and reptiles increase. In the central part of the day, the seasonal rains are
delivered. In the third part of the day, diseases are indicated. Otherwise,
continuous rainfall day and night is beneficial for the rainy seasonal
wealth. Rain in the first part of the night is generally good. In the second
part, rainfall is good for cereals. In the third part, it is beneficial for
summer crops. Rains in the fourth part are destructive for all herbs.
