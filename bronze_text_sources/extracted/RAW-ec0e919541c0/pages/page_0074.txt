MAU is the phrase ardhantam, meaning the end-of-the-half. This technical
word clearly refers to the middle but when Sun’s movement is considered
the text likes to make it clear that it is the end of the second quarter. Thus
besides individual visible stars the concept of equal divisions using some
Support points was in vogue during Vedic times, which was inherited by PT.
