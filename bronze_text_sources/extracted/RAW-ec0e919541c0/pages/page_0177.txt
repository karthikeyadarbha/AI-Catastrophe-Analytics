In AS there is a long chapter called Rksadyadbhuta, which
roughly translates as wonders-of-stars. This contains astral-
geography and much more, including affliction of stars by other
bodies. The statements on dividing India into nine directional
roups controlled by three stars each are almost same as the

02

ones quoted by Utpala. Hence we are not repeating them
here. However, AS under Rksddyadbhuta contains detailed
description of the 27 naksatras, each as a group of one or
more stars. Portents associated with their individual and group
(occultation) afflictions are also stated. Since recognition of the
canonical nava-grahas (nine-graspers) was due to their capacity
to afflict or cover up the stars, we give this text as the next
chapter of PT.]
