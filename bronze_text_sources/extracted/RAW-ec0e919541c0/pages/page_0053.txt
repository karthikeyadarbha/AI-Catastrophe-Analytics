1.9 [About Saura that is Saturn]

In the beginning of creation all living beings were affected by the
extraordinary heat of Vivasvan. Then the Creator told Vivasvan to reduce
his heat. Ravi (Sun) so ordained, being angry at the reduction of his heat,
generated an offspring who is known as Sanaiscara.
