Max Mueller while translating the Maitrayani Aranyaka Upanisad
(MAU 7.6) of the Krsna Yajurveda takes the words Rahu and Ketu as

existence of the knowledge of lunar nodes in MAU pre-empting proper
dating of its contents since the concept of Moon’s orbit intersecting the
ecliptic at two imaginary points in space is evidenced only in Sanskrit
astronomy texts appearing at the begining of the Common Era.

death’. This meaning has to be rejected in the overall context of a “peace
hymn’ to = celestial objects.
