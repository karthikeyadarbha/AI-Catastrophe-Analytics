11.14 At the end of the effects of Svetaketu (that is after 10 years)

Padmaketu rises in the west with its crown coloured like a lotus stalk.

Moving one night, it brings immense happiness for a period of seven years.

11.13 Kasyapa Svadhiketu is seen, 115 years after the transit of Padmaketu,
in star Jyestha. It is dark and harsh occupying one-third of the sky. It
recedes anti-clockwise, with a crown rotating clockwise above, like a lock
of hairs. For years equal to the number of months seen, it reduces the
population of the noble groups in the middle region and north to one-third.

[Utpala’s text mentions this comet again as Svetaketu instead of
as svadhiketu. This obviously is a scribal mistake in the Varanasi
BS edition by Dwivedi. Vrddhagarga says that this comet rises

in the star Sravana.]
