14.2 [ Ballalasena ~ Now follows the ill effects of individual and combined
afflictions of stars, as per Parasara.|

Now (we describe) afflictions of stars. Unnatural combination with
Moon, entering of Sun (invisibility due to Sun following the star), mixing
of the rays at rise and set of planets, shifting from its (regular) place, halos,
contact with meteors, loss of rays (brightness) and change in colour; are
the afflictions. We explain for each star individually the effects of such
affliction.
