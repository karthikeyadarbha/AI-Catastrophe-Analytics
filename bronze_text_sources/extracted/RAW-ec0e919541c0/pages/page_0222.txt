17.10 [Un AS: Parasara about wind anomaly]

Winds happen in the eight directions. Among them pleasant, fragrant and
breezy wind is always good. The wind in the east is responsible for cloud
production. The one from west destroys clouds in the months of Nabha
and Nabhasya (rainy season). Northern wind is conducive to daily rains.
