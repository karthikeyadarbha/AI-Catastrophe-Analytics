9.1 [07 Utpala's commentary]

Venus has three routes north, central and south as prescribed. These are
each further divided into three to get nine routes. In the north are Naga,
Gaja, Airdvata paths. In the centre are Vrsabha, Go and Jaradgavya
paths. In the south are Mrga, Aja and Dahana paths. Among these Krttika,
Bharani and Svati are the Naga-vithi. The three stars Rohini, Mrgasira,
Ardra are the Gaja-vithi. The next four stars (Punarvasu, Pusya, Aslesa,
Magha) are the Airavati. The two Phalgunis are the Vrsabha-(vithi). The
four stars Parvabhadra, Uttarabhadra, Revati, Asvini are the Govithi.
Sravana, Dhanistha, Satabhisaj are Jaradgava. Citra and Hasta are the
Mrgavithi. Ajavithi is Anuradha, Visakha, and Jyestha. Mila, Pirvasadha

and Uttarasadha are the Vaisvanara-vithi.
