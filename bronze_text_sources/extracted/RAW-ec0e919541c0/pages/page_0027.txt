Three published sources are available for compiling the statements
attributed to Parasara. These are;

1) Commentary of Utpala edited by K. C. Dvivedi (Brhat Samhita of
Varaha Mihira with Sanskrit commentary; Sampurnananda Sanskrit
University, Varanasi. 1996).

1) The Adbhutasdgara edited by Muralidhar Jha (Adbhuta Sagara of
Ballala Sena, Sanskrit Text, Prabhakari & Co, Benares Cantt. 1905).

iii) Commentary of Bhaskarayogi edited by K.V.Sarma (Brhat Samhita
of Varaha Mihira with the commentary Utpala-parimala of Yogisvara;
Rashtriya Sanskrit Sansthan, N.Delhi 2007).
