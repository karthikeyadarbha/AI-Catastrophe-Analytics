carried out under the different tithis. Here the tithis are referred

16.3 The residing deities of the tithis are Brahma, Aamaramantri,
Janardana, Yama, Soma, Kumara, Muni, Vasu, Pisact, Dharma, Rudra,

Ravi, Kama, Rudra & Pitrs.

[Like naksatras, tithis have their regent deities. Here, the fifteen

tithis are assigned in sequential order to the named deities

Jupiter assigned to the second tithi is here called Amaramantri
the minister of gods. The sixth ¢ithi is governed by Kumara
who has six faces. The seventh appropriately has Muni referring
to the Seven Sages or U.Major. Similarly, the eighth is assigned

to Vasu who are eight in number. ]
