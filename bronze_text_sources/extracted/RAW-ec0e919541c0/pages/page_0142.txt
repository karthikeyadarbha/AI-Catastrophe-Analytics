11.18 Samvartaka is seen, after a lapse of 108 years, in the evening after
the Sun has set in the west. It occupies one-third of the sky, with a thin
dreadful copper coloured spear-like head, ejecting a jet of smoke. For
years equal to the muhiirtas of its stay, kings fight among themselves.

Whichever star it covers with its smoke, the countries associated with
those stars are troubled.
