15.8 Sun, Mercury, Jupiter and Saturn are Nagara (city dwellers). Moon,
Mars, Venus, Rahu and Ketu are travelers. Depending on the victory,
defeat and balance between the members of the two groups of planets,
there will be victory, defeat and balance between city dwellers and nomads

(or migrants) in the country.
