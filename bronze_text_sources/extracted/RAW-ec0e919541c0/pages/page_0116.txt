9.3 Adya, Rohita, Daruna, Virocana, Urdhva danda, Tiksna are the six
mandalas (spatial circular regions). The first four are formed of four stars
each, starting with Bharani. The other two (mandalas) are formed with
five stars each, starting with Jyestha. The first four are also called Vata.

Tiksna is also known as Vyadamarga. The Urdhvadanda without star
Sravana is called Vaisvanara.
