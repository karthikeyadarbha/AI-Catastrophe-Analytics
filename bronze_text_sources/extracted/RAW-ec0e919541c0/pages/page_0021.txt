Note to the reader:

Due to software dependent type setting of the Sanskrit text, line justification becomes difficult
with long composite words as depicted in the source books. Hence, in some places where sandhi
is optional the words are resolved such that the preceding word ends with either the anusvara or
the ardhaksara © when the succeeding word starts with a vowel. Where the words can not be

broken hyphens are used to indicate continuation.

Examples for astronomical negative years:
Year -1500=1499 BCE; 1350BCE= Year -1351
