can be detected for the author of the text under study. Hence the PT
text need not be taken to be by a single author belonging to a particular
period. It follows, any statement about the date of astronomer Parasara
should be taken as the traceable initial period of the oral tradition of
Pardsaratantra, same as that of its originator, but not of the text PT fixed
in script. Nevertheless, in delineating the historical development of Hindu
