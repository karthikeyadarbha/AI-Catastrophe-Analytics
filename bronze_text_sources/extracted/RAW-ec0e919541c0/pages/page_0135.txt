11.8 Kaliketu, born out of Rudra’ anger during the sacrifice of Daksa,
rises after 300 years and 9 months. From the east, in the Vaisvanara path,
with a harsh copper-red colour head like the tip of a trident, it travels one-
third (or three parts) of the sky to be seen at the horizon. For years, equal
to the number of months seen, the comet having reduced the population to
one-third, leaves the autumn grain yield to be worth eight measures.
