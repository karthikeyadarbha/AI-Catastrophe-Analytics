20.4 [This long text describes the earthly result for the third type
of dreams which are seen in one way but are to be understood
differently. Interpretations are given for kings and also for
lay people. The last section above refers to dreams which are
supposed to indicate death. Some of these statements, such
as riding a camel southward in dream as a death portent, are
prevalent even now.]
