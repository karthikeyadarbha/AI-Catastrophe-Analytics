ii. Deviation due to the Moon’s course

The maximum declination, multiplied by the versine
of the Moon’s kofi and divided by Trijyd, gives the devia-
tion due to the Moon’s course. Here, the direction is
always the direction of the course: (69)

For the planet is in its northward course (when in the

otherwise. The (maximum) deviation is when (itis) at
the ends of the (southern and northern) hemispheres,

At the ends of the hemispheres (7.¢., at the first points

tit. “Total deviation

Find the two arcs of deviation and add them together

are of the same or different directions. Find the sine,
multiply it by the diameter of the Moon or the Sun
