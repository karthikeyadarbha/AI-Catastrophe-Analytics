Immersion and Emergence

Colour of the eclipse

If Moon is eclipsed partially it is smoky in colour;
if half is eclipsed it is black and a full eclipse is reddish
brown. The solar eclipse is always balck in colour. (6b)

To represent the amount of obscuration graphically,
draw a circle of radius equal to the semi-diameter of the

the point marking its end as centre, draw a circle of
