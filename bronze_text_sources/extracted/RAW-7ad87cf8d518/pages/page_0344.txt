Sine and cosine

(Ina circle), three Signs make a quadrant. In the
odd quadrants, the arc traversed is called bhuja (or
bahu) and that to be traversed is called kofi; in the even
quadrants, the two are, respectively, called the kofi and
bahu (or bhuja). This is the convention. (10-29). (KVS)

R sine table (geometrically)

Divide the quadrant of the circumference of a circle
(into as many parts as desired). Then, from (right)
triangles and quadrilaterals, one can find as may R sines
of equal arcs as one likes, for any given angle (and thus
prepare the R sine table).1 (11). (KSS)
