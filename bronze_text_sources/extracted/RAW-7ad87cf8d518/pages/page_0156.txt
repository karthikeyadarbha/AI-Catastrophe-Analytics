It has been said by the ancient sages that the positions
of nuksatras, Asvini etc., in their respective portions are,
respectively, 48’, 40’, 56’, 54’, 52, 20’, 72’, 70’, 44’, 48’,
36, 44’, 78’, 66’, 62’, 72’, 52’, 8, 6’, 4’, 4’, 19’, 18’, 40’,
42८, 12’, and 74’, each multiplied by 10.

They also say that the polar longitude of each naksatra
is equal in minutes to the sum of its portion and 800
times the number of the naksatras preceding it (that is,
its own number less 1).1 (1-3). (BC).
