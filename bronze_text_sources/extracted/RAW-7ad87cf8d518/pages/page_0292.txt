The Sun’s R sine amplitude is its bahu (bhuja). If the
baku (bhuja) of the Moon is in a direction different from

same, then take their difference. The result in cach
case is called the ‘corrected bahw’ (bhuja) ov sphutabdhu.

In the first case, its direction is the same as that of the
Moon’s bahu (bhuja). In the second case, its direction
is the same as that of the Moon’s bahu (bhkuja) if the

Again, ifit is daytime, find the difference of the R sine

The square root of the sum of the squares of the prathama

Now, they say that the Moon’s R sine altitude is the
perpendicular. The square root of the sum of the
squares of the base (bahu) and perpendicular (Kofi) is
