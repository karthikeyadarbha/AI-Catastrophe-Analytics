Eccentric method

The second method is called prativrttabhangi or method
of concentric and eccentric. This method supposes
that the mean Sun or Moon moves with a uniform
velocity along its circular orbit or concentric and the
true Sun moves with the same velocity and in the same
direction along the circumference of an equal circle
called prativrita or eccentric, the centre of which is
situated on the line joining the apogee to the centre of the
Earth, at a distance equal to mandéntyaphalajya or radius
of the epicycle of the body, from the latter point.
