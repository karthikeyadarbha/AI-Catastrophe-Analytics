Direct computation of R sines

(Now) I briefly state the rule (for finding the bhujaphala
and the kotiphala, etc.) without making use of the R sine-
differences, 225, etc. Subtract the degrees of the bhuja
(or Kofi) from the degrees of half a circle (i.e. 180 degrees).
Then multiply the remainder by the degrees of the bhuja
(or kofi) and put down the result at two places. At one
place subtract the result from 40,500. By one-fourth of
the remainder (thus obtained) divide the result at the
other place as multiplied by the antyaphala (i.e. the
epicyclic radius). Thus is obtained the entire baihuphala
(or kotiphala) for the Sun, Moon or the star-planets. So
are also obtained the direct and inverse ए sines.!
(17-19). (KSS)
