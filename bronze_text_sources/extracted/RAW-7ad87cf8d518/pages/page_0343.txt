Correction to visibility correction

The wise men have laid down the rule that to a planet,
both on the eastern and western horizons, visibility
correction due to deviation of the ecliptic or dyanadrkkar-
mdsu and visibility correction due to the terrestrial
latitude or dksadrkkarmadsu should be applied. The
reason is somewhat similar to that for the application of
ascensional difference. (48).

When the latitudes (of two plancts) are equal in
magnitude and direction, the planets have the same
day-circle. But when one planet has a latitude smaller
than that of the other planet, it appears in a direction
opposite to that of the latitude (with regard to the centre
of the other planet) even if the latitudes are of the same
denomination. (49). (BC)
