Multiply the interval that has elapsed or yet to elapse
(from sunrise or any standard time) by the daily motion
of the planet. Divide by 60. The result in degrees is
to be added to the position of the planet, if the pre-
vious interval is yet to elapse, it should be subtracted
otherwise. The result gives the position of the planet.
