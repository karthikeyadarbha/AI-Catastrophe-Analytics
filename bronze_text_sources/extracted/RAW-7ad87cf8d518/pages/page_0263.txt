In a lunar eclipse, the contact takes place in the
eastern, portion of the disc of the Moon and separation
in the western portion. The contrary is the case in
a solar eclipse.

The latitudes of the Moon should always be drawn in
a direction contrary to their own (in the projection)
of a lunar eclipse. (But in the projection) of a solar
eclipse, the latitudes should be drawn in their own
direction. (29)
