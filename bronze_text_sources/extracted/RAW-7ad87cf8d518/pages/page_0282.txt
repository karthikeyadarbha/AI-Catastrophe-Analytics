Conversion of minutes into angulas

In order that the graphical representation may appear
as the eclipse is seen actually, the minutes of arc are to be
converted into digits, at 2’ per digit when the Moon is
near the horizon, and at 3’ per digit when it is on the

Colour of the eclipse
At the beginning and end of its eclipse, the Moon
(i.e., the obscured part of the Moon) is smoky; when
half obscured, it is black; when (just) totally obscured,
(i.e. at immersion), it is tawny; when far inside the
Shadow, it is copper-coloured with blackish tinge.
(46). (KSS)
