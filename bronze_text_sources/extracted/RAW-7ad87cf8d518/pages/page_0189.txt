From the Kali day for which the planet is desired,
deduct the respective sodhya given in the tables. Divide
the remainder by the respective mandalas (any mandala
or mandalas may be used, and any number of times) and
note the quotients. Divide the remainder by the
respective synodic cycle of days. The quotients are the
cycles gone, and the remainder, the days in the next
cycle. (i) Find the total dkruva in the manner to be
given. (ii) Using the mmemonics of that cycle,
corrected in the manner to be given, take values for the
maximum number of days that can be done. (ii) Find
the motion for the remaining days by interpolation.
Add (i), (ii) and (iii), (iii being deductive if the motion
is found to be retrograde.) The (unreduced) True
planet is got. (1-2)
