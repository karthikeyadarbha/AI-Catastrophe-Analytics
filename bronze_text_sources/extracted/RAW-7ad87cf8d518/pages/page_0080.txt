4. 28. 1

Circle of the sky and the orbits of planets

Multiply the revolutions of the Moon (in a yuga) by
32,40,000 and then discard the zero at the unit’s place;
(this is the length of the circle of the sky in terms of
yojanas).

(Severally) divide that by the revolutions of the
planets (in a_yuga); thus are obtained the lengths of the
orbits of the respective planets in terms of yojanas.
(35).4 (KSS)
