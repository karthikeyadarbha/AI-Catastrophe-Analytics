Naksatra computation

For every 800 minutes of arc in the Moon’s longitude
there is one naksatra (asterismal segment). Deduct the
Sun’s longitude from the Moon’s. For every twelve
degrees of the remainder there is one tithi. The time
of the ending moment of the naksatra should be found
by proportion using the Moon’s daily motion. The time
of the ending moment of the tithi should be found by
proportion using the difference in the daily motions of
the Sun and the Moon. (16) (TSK)
