Basis for zero-corrections

From the elapsed Kali days (for any desired date)
deduct (a khanda, a lump number of days, equal to)
15,02,008 (dinanamranusasya). Multiply the remainder
(khandasesa) by 6845 (fivaditta) and divide by 1,68,611
(parydptahrdaya) 1 The quotient (got is called dhruva-
sadhana and) is to be used for deriving the several zerc-
corrections (dhruvas) to be used for calculations which
will be enunciated below. The first dhruva (is to be
calculated) using this quotient itself (agrimaphala), while
the further eight (dhruvas) are to be derived from this
dhruva reduced increasingly by 1. (5-6)

Moon-sentence numbers

Divide the remainder (in the division in 5) by 6845
(sivadiita). The quotient obtained will be the first
Moon-sentence-number (vakyasankhya).2 The further
(eight sentence-numbers can be obtained) in the same
manner from the division by 6842 (fivadita) of the
successive remainders to which 1,88,611 (paryaptahrdaya)
has been added. (7)
