The tabular R sines by computation ।

To get the R sines successively previous to the one
already found, multiply this R sine by twice the very
last difference, and divide by the radius. The result
plus the difference (using which this R sine has been

found) is the difference to be subtracted from this R
sine to get the next previous R sine.!
