Eclipses, Rahu and Ketu

The mythological view of Rahu devouring the Sun or the Moon,
thus causing the eclipse, was current among the superstitious laymen
and even among some astrologers cum astronomers. However, in
the fifth century A.D. Aryabhata I gave anexplanation for the eclipses
in terms of the Sun being obscured by the Moon, and the shadow of the
Earth obscuring the Moon. His junior contemporary, Varahamihira
categorically stated that a lunar eclipse is caused by the entry of the
Moon into the shadow of the Earth. (See below, pp. 196-97). Even
so, he maintained the nomenclature of Rdhu, stating that the ascending
node is Rahu’s head, and the descending node, Rahu’s tail. Later the
word #दांढ began to be used for connoting the nodes. Yet, the word
Rahu and Ketu are used in the technical sense of ascending and de-
scending nodes in a number of astronomical texts. In some of the
religious texts, the word Ketu also means cither a comet or an earthly
activity caused by the Sun
