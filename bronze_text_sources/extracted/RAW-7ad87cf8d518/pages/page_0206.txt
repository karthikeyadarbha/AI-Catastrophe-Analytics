Equation of the centre for the Sun and the Moon

Diminish the mean longitude ofthe Sun or the Moon by
that of its own apogee, and obtain the R sine of the bahu

thereof. In the case of the Sun, divide that by 140;

and in the case of the Moon, divide that by 60. The

resulting degrees should be subtracted from the (respec-
tive) mean longitude (ofthe Sun or the Moon) or added to
that, according as the Sun or the Moon isin the northern
part (of the anomalistic sphere) or in the southern part.

Thus are obtained the true longitudes of the Sun and the

Moon. (25-26a)

Types of planetary motion
The motion of the planets is of eight kinds: retrograde
(vakrd), somewhat retrograde (anuvakra@), transverse
(kutil@), slow (manda), very slow (mandatara), even (sama),
also vey swift (sighratara), and swift (Sighrd). (12)

Of these, the very swift (atifighra), that called swift,
the slow, the very slow, the even—all these five are forms
of the motion called direct (४) ; the ‘somewhat retro-
grade’ is retrograde. (13). (Burgess)
