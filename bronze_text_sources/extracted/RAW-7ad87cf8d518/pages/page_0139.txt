Yasti-yantra (The graduated tube)

The Yasti-yantra! which is equal in length to the semi-
diameter of the (perfect) circle, with as many gradua-
tions of angulas as there are degrees in a radian (i.e. 57),
should be held at the centre of the circle towards
the Sun. The yasti then denotes the hypotenuse, its
elevation denotes the gnomon, and the distance from
the foot of the gnomon up to the centre of the circle
always denotes the shadow (of the gnomon.) The
degrees intervening between the end of the yasti and
the rising point of the Sun, divided by six, give the
ghatis elapsed in the day. (8b-10a). (1९88)

Distance between two planets and their longitudes. Determine
the distance between the two planets with the help of
the gnomon or the Yasti, like that between the Sun and
the Moon. This added to (the longitude of) the west-

mark the north-south and east-west lines. From their
