Number of stars in the asterisms

are:) 6 for Krttika; 1 for Rohini; 3 for Mrgasiras; 1 for
Ardra; 2 for Punarvasi; 1 for Pusya; 6 for Aglesa; 6 for
Magha; 4 for the Phalgunis (2 for Pairva-phalguni and
2 for Uttara-phalguni); 5 for Hasta; 1 for Citra; 1 for
Svati; 2 for Visakha; 4 for Anuradha; 1 for Jyestha;
7 for Mila; 8 for the Asadhas (7 for Pirva-asadha; and
1 for Uttara-asadh4); 1 for Abhijit; 3 for Sravana; 5 for
Sravistha; 1 for Satabhisak; 4 for the Prosthapadas;
(2 for Pirva-prosthapada and 2 Uttara-prosthapada) ;
1 for Revati; 2 for Agvayuk (Agvini) ; and 3 for Bharani.
Thus are the constellations limited in the number of
