Day-time (Paulisa)

To find the day-time in Mesa, Vrsabha and Mithuna,
add the cara differences one by one, in the order given, to
30 nédikads and in the next three, subtract in the reverse
order. In the next three rasis, Tula, etc., again subtract
from 30 nadis in the given order, and for Makara etc.,
add in the reverse order. This will give the day-time
fairly accurately for places in Northern (whole) India.
I shall give the method to find the day-time accurately
in other places (in the fourth chapter) when dealing
with spherical astronomy.) (11-12). (TSK)
