17. 5. 2.

उदये सौम्यविक्षेपे देयमस्तमये सदा |
व्यस्तं तद्याम्यविक्षेपे कार्य स्यादुदयास्तयोः ।। २ ॥
(Bhaskara I, LBh., 6. 1-2)

_Multiply the R sine of the Moon’s latitude by the
R sine of the (local) latitude and divide (the product)
by the R sine of the colatitude. Whatever is thus
obtained should be subtracted from the Moon’s longitude
in the case of the rising of the Moon (i.e., in the eastern
hemisphere) and added to that in the case of the setting
of the Moon (i.e., in the western hemisphere), provided
that the Moon’s latitude is north. When the Moon’s
latitude is south, the above correction is applied reversely
in the cases of rising and setting (both). (1-2). (KSS)

17. 5. 6. एवं कमंक्रमात्‌ सिद्धो दुष्यतेऽन्तरितः शशी ।

(Bhaskara I, LBh., 6. 3-5)

When the Moon obtained by applying these (two
visibility) corrections is found to be twelve degrees (of
time) distant from the Sun, she shall be (just) visible
in clear cloudless sky. (3). (KSS)
