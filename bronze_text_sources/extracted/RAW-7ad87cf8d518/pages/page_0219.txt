—True Planets

The mandaphala of Venus is the same as that of the
Sun. The mandaphala of the son of the Moon, (that is, of
Mercury), is twice that of the Sun. The mandaphala of
Mars is 5 times that of the Sun. The mandaphala of
Jupiter is 1% times that of the Sun and doubled. The
mandaphala of Saturn is 17% times that of the sun and
quadrupled. (6b-7)
