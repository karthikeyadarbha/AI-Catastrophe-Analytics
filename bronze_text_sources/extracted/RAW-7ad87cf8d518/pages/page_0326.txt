True Moon
The sum of the (relevant) vdkyas (Moon-sentences)
and the (relevant) dhruvas will give the True Moon-s
(at those vakyakala-s). The True Moon (for times) in
between (two vdkyakala-s) will have be calculated by
interpolation. The Moon’s motion during an interval
is the difference between the two (relevant) True Moons
(and hence the said interpolation). (35b-36)
