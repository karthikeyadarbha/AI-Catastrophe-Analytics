People on the equator see the north polar star on the

For the people on Meru, i.e. at the North pole, the

Meru mountain

The Meru (mountain) is exactly one yojana (in
height). It is light-producing, surrounded by the
Himavat mountain, situated in the middle of the
Nandana forest, made of jewels, and cylindrical in
shape. (11)

The heaven and the Meru mountain are at the centre
of the land (i.e., at the North pole); the hell and the
Badavamukha are at the centre of the water (7.¢., at the
South pole). The gods (residing at the Meru mountain)
and the demons (residing at the Badavamukha)
consider themselves positively and permanently below
each other. (12). (55)
