He (the Sun) never sets or rises. When (men) think
that he is setting, he is only turning round, after reaching
the end of the day, and makes night here and day below.
Then, when (men) think he is rising in the morning, he
is only turning round after reaching the end of the night,
and makes day here and night below. Thus, he (the
Sun) never sets at all. (6)
