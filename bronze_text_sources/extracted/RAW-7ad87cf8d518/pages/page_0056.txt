Take as the first case the following instance :

‘On Kali day 16,43,524, (at a solar eclipse), the
gnomonic shadow at commencement was eleven and
the end (of the eclipse) occurred in the afternoon.’

Here the ahargana referred to is at sunrise preceding
the eclipse. The mean Sun then is 0 11° 57° 15",
correct to seconds. The mean Moon correct to minutes
is 77 4° 15’. The Moon’s Higher Apsis is 7 7° 14’.

The commencement of the eclipse is to be tested first,
since it has been stated that ‘the gnomonic shadow is
eleven at commencement.’ There too the process of
successive approximation has to be done. The rough
time elapsed after sunrise might be understood from the
measure of the shadow. That comes to 5 nadikis, 40
vinadikas. Now, at Aévatthagrama, on the bank of river
Nila, the desadntara (longitudinal time difference) is 20
vinadis, positive. The Equinoctial shadow is 2 afigulas
and 18 vyangulas. The sum of deSantara and time
elapsed after sunrise is 6 nadis. (The longitude for)
the motion thereof should be added to the longitude at
sunrise. The mean Sun would then be 7 12° 3’ 12”.
This is not for six nddis after the local sunrise but for
that of the ghatikdmandala at the western horizon at
180° from Lanka at the junction at the local N-S line,
the horizon and the Equatorial E-W line. This.is from
the rise at a place having the longitude of mean Sun,
but not from the time of sunrise. The difference in
motion in terms of prénas of mean Sun and the corres-
ponding true Sun has now to be applied in toto to the
mean, it being positive if the true pranas are greater,
and negative if less. The true prdnas are calculated for
the first quadrant using the formula enunciated in the
verse istajydgunita etc. and added to six. In the second
quadrant it has to be subtracted from six rasts. In the
third, it is to be added to six क. In the fourth, it
is to be subtracted from twelve rafis. In places having
latitude (i.e. north or south of the equator), pranas equal
to half local ascensional difference have to be subtracted
from the true pranas if (the Sun is in the six rasis)
beginning from Aries and added if beginning from
Libra. The result would indicate the rising point of
the ghatikimandala at sunrise. The difference in
minutes between this point and the mean Sun would be
in pranas. In all cases these have to be multiplied by
their respective rates of motion and divided by 21,600
and the resultant minutes applied to the respective
