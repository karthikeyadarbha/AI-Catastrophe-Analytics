Academic genealogy of Govinda of Kerala

Govinda is a Bhattatiri of the Talakkulattir family
(of nampitiri brahmins). His date of birth is Kali
15,84,362 (in A.D. 1237). His ancestral house is near
Keralesvara (in north Kerala). (1)

Paramegvara, (the grandson of Govinda’s pupil)?
is a nampitiri of the Vatasseri family. ParameSvara
resided on the northern bank of the Nila river (in north
Kerala). (2)

His son is Damodara. (3)

His pupil is Nilakantha Somayaji. He is the author
of Tantrasangraha, Aryabhatiyabhdsya and other works.
His date is known from the Kali day 16,80,553 (in A.D.
1500). (4)

Of the aforesaid Damodara, Jyesthadeva is the pupil.
He is a nampitiri of the Parankottu (family). It was
he who wrote the work Yuktibhasé. (3)

The pupil of Jyesthadeva is Acyvta Pisarati belonging
to Trkkantiyir (in Central Kerala). He is the author
of Sphutanirnaya, Goladipika and other works 2 (6)

I, (Krsnadasa or Krsnan Agan), worship the fect of
my father and teacher widely known everywhere as
Raman (Aégan), famed as a promulgator of the name
of god Rama and teacher of even teachers of Jyotisa. (1)

I adore the teacher of my teacher, most compassionate
and resident of Vyaghra-mukha-mandira (viz. Puli-
mukhattu Potti, in Malayalam). (2)

I adore also his (viz. Potti’s) teacher, the great
intellectual, thought-reader, noted astronomer and of
unimpaired reputation, Navayikkulattu Azhati. (3)
