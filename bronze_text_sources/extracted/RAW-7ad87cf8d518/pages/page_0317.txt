Time of star-planet conjunction
The dyana-drkkarma is to be done (with respect to the

20. 6. 1

planet) and the sphutaSara is to be computed to know the
time of (polar latitudinal) conjunction. (9)

The difference in the longitudes of the planet and the
star, divided by the daily motion of the planet, gives the
number of days approximately after or before the
moment of conjunction! (10)

If the planet be retrograde, the conjunction past
or future will be in the reverse, i.e. future or

past. (11). (AS)
